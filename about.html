OUR STORY

Started small. Built on accountability.

Keenum Lawncare and Landscape Solutions is a locally owned team passionate about top-notch lawn and landscape care, with a reputation for excellence, reliability and customer satisfaction across the Fort Myers community.

MEET THE OWNER

Mason Keenum

Five years ago, Mason started out small. Rather than focusing on one specific service, he realized it was easiest for customers to have one company that does it all, and that’s the business he’s been building ever since.

Today he leads the team on every project, from designs to installations to maintenance. Having lived in Florida his whole life, he brings real expertise in the lawns and landscapes that thrive here, and helps turn every home into its own oasis.
